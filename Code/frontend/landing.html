❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is Magnetite Vision? ▼
Magnetite Vision is an advanced AI-powered computer vision application engineered by Ben Yahia for real-time automatic license plate recognition (ALPR) and commercial parking supervision.
What AI models are used under the hood? ▼
It utilizes YOLOv8 fine-tuned on ELPD Commercial v2 (3.2M parameters) paired with a Multi-Engine OCR pipeline (EasyOCR and PaddleOCR) featuring character-level consensus voting.
Is the platform RGPD / GDPR compliant? ▼
Yes, it fully adheres to data protection guidelines with automated 30-day data retention purging, access controls, and JWT-authenticated sessions.
Who developed Magnetite Vision? ▼
Developed by Mohamed Walid Ben Yahia (ENSA Tétouan State Engineer in Big Data & AI) for company Ben Yahia in partnership with Flowdesigns Solutions.
